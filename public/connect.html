Data dari jam / band Anda akan dikirim otomatis ke sistem kesiapan atlet Timnas:

  • tidur, detak jantung (istirahat, rata-rata, maksimal), HRV

Your watch / band data (sleep, heart rate, HRV, steps, activity, stress, SpO2, breathing, recovery) is sent to the national team's readiness system. Only Coach Donny and admins can read it. Your password never reaches us: you log in on WHOOP's or Garmin's own page. You can disconnect at any time.
