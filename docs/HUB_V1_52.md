# Hub V1.52 — pain map: hamstring and calf on the back figure (24 Nov 2026)

**The support team's ask (WhatsApp, 24 Nov):** on the back of the body, add points on the left and right where the
arrows were drawn: the back of the thigh and the lower leg.

**What changed:** four new points on the back figure of the physio pain map.
- **Hamstring (paha belakang)**, left and right: the back of the thigh
- **Betis (calf)**, left and right: the back of the lower leg

They work like every other point: tap to pick, a pain score 0–10, listed under "Atau pilih titik", counted in the
summary maps. Only physios see the pain map (as since V1.50). Reports already saved are unchanged.

**Tests:** `tests/painmap_v152.test.js` (the points, labels in Bahasa and English, left/right placement, saving).
