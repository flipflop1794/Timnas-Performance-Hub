# Timnas Performance Hub V1.5 — Competitors tab

Prepared 3 Nov 2026. Builds on V1.4. Not published: files only.

New top-level **Competitors** tab (the per-title competitor boards stay inside each game):
- **All nations**: every nation in our seven WEC fields, ranked by staff "main threat" tiers, then how many of our titles they enter, then notes. Shows tier per title, note and VOD counts, stand-in scrims done, last update. eFootball-only nations (96-nation entry list) are hidden until someone scouts them.
- **Nation profile**: one nation across every title we meet it in, with tier and reason, notes with sources, VODs (reviewed or not), sparring partners that play like it, stand-in scrims, and WEC matches and game notes. Links back to the title page to add notes.
- **Scouting coverage**: per title, field size, nations with notes, tiers set, VODs reviewed, and three nations to scout next. Unscouted multi-title nations come first, so one piece of work helps several coaches.
- "Titles at WEC" counts are now computed from the published fields. They had come from the 29 Oct list, which predates eFootball, so Egypt now shows 6.

Nothing is scored or predicted. 53 automated tests pass; desktop and phone layouts were checked.
