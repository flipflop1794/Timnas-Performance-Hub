# Timnas Performance Hub V1.12 — polish before launch

1. **Safer team editing (website).** Every save carries the version the device last loaded. If a colleague saved the same
   record in between, the save is refused: "Not saved: Aufa Bassam changed this record at 18:02 WIB, after you opened it…".
   What you typed stays on screen; the page shows their version. Covers objectives, scrim plans, scouting, opponent cards,
   match cards, plans, wellbeing and shared configuration (runbooks, rules). Scrim reports were already insert-only.
2. **Phone app feel (website).**
   - Title coaches and analysts see only their own games in the menu and overviews.
   - A title coach opening the site lands directly on his game's Scrim reports.
   - Add to home screen: app name "Timnas Hub", icon, dark status bar (Android and iPhone).
3. **Report trends (both versions).** Each Scrim reports tab shows the last 14 days: win rate by day (points for PUBG Mobile)
   and plan followed by day (1–5), as two separate charts. Hover or tap a day for its numbers.
