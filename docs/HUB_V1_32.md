# Hub V1.32 — less noise for coaches (18 Nov 2026)

Website only; no database or AI change. Nothing is deleted: screens are merged or hidden, and every old link still
opens its page.

## 1. Fewer tabs per title (10–12 → 6–7)
- **Analysis** replaces Trends, Weaknesses and Patterns in the tab bar; the three open from small links at the
  top of the Analysis page.
- The per-title **Competitors** tab is gone from MLBB, MLBB Women, eFootball, CS2, CS2 Women and PUBG Mobile. Its
  notes, tiers, VODs and results now live under **Competitors → Notes per title** (pick the title at the top).
  Dota 2 keeps its own Competitors tab (the OpenDota scouting is only there).
- On the website, screens not needed before WEC stay hidden for everyone signed in (Juara Umum board, Is it
  working?, Between games, Dota Post-match / Draft room / Series review, PUBG Mobile Strategy sim, wellbeing
  check-in screens when Coach Donny's system is on). **Show all tools** in the top menu brings them back. The
  offline hub file still shows everything.

## 2. One way to log a session
- Planned sessions show **Log the result (scrim report)** only. The old "Log result" mini-form is gone; a small
  **Did not take place? → Mark as cancelled** stays.
- The report takes over what that form did: it closes the planned session and, when the session had objectives,
  asks for one result each (met / partly met / not met / not measured, plus a measured value) and records it in
  the coaching cycle.
- The **Log a session** buttons on Scrims & practice and Scrim reports are gone (the report is the daily session
  report). Today → Daily sessions is unchanged.

## 3. Shorter wording
- Long method notes now sit behind **How to read this** (Trends verdict, players table, staff changes, CS2 map
  pool, plan-followed rating, Scrims & practice).
- The report form intro is one line; the rules (one report per partner, saved reports are fixed, performance notes
  only) are one line above the Save bar.
- Title coaches no longer see setup messages they cannot act on (screenshot reading not switched on, Draft Room
  database update); admins still do.

## 4. Simpler Today for title coaches
Title coaches (no admin, Head Coach, assistant or readiness role) see: Needs attention now, sessions coming up
(with Log the result), scrim reports, and their title's objectives. End of day, the review queue, Pelatnas and
rival roster changes stay on the Head Coach's and admins' Today and the Main dashboard.

## Guide
"File the daily scrim report", "Start here", Patterns and Competitors paths updated (EN and ID).
